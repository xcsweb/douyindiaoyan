About this template

What this template is for

Modern text-and-image layouts, text emphasis and information breakdowns accompany a talking-head video. Suitable for arguments, evidence and step-by-step explanations.

Format and examples

This is a 16:9 landscape template. The website provides full demos in Chinese and English. The examples show the visual style and pacing; use your own content and assets for a new video.

What you can change

Provide your own talking-head video, script or SRT, then choose suitable motion for text emphasis, comparisons, steps, evidence, summaries and endings. Component colors, materials, sizes and positions should fit the template's visual style and the person's safe area. Landscape and portrait use independently composed versions.

Materials to prepare

Prepare the talking-head video and original audio, a matching script or subtitles, and the images, footage and data referenced in this video. You can start with only a script to discuss the visual plan; production requires the real voice-over duration and confirmed assets. If you have not provided footage of a person, the example person will not be used in your place.

Animation and components

The extracted native animation and adaptation contract are retained, alongside a motion-kit 0.3.0 workflow with 70 controls. Use the separate entry point for added components, choose them according to the script's meaning and check that they match this template's visual style. Later component-library upgrades arrive through new template releases.

Check the talking-head video, subtitles and assets against their actual timing; the example duration does not limit a new script. If titles are too long, evidence is missing or music does not cover the full video, adjust the content or supply the missing assets before exporting.

Use and updates

Give the .motion file to an assistant that supports the format. Read the guide and material contract first, plan in a separate project, review the actual preview and then export. Example assets, fonts and brands each follow their bundled licenses. Online voice generation and similar services are optional and depend on available capabilities, pricing and authorization. Template updates follow the versions published on the website.

License

Template code retains the original author's MIT License. Fonts, brands, likenesses, example images, audio and other third-party assets follow their separate bundled licenses. MIT does not grant rights to those assets that have not been explicitly licensed.
